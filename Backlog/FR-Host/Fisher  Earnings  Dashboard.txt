Screen definition :

Dashboard: KPI cards — Earnings This Week | This Month | Pending | Total Lifetime; line chart 12-month earnings; table: Order ID | Buyer | Amount | Commission | Net | Date

User story :

As a fisher/exporter, I want to see my earnings so I can track income and plan finances

Acceptance criteria:

i. The chart should initially load dummy data.

ii.Data should refresh every 5 minutes.

iii. Data should be exportable to CSV.
