Screen definition :

Form: star rating (1-5) overall + sub-ratings (Quality, Communication, Timeliness, Packaging), written review textarea, [Submit] button — unlocked after order Delivered

User story :

As a buyer, I want to rate my trade partner so the community maintains quality standards

Acceptance criteria :

i. Review unlocks should only post-delivery
ii. A written review should contain a minimum of 250 characters.
iii. Only one review should be submitted per order.
